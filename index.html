    How every lab works

    1. See it move

      Every idea starts as a live simulation or graph you can drag, before any formulas.

    2. Predict, then check

      Guess what will happen, run it, and see why. Worked examples go one step at a time.

    3. Practice until it sticks

      Randomized problems, an exam builder and a mistakes queue that brings back what you missed.

    Install the Intulab app

    It's free, with no app store. It opens full screen from your home screen, and once you've opened it, it works offline too.

    iPhone or iPad (Safari)

    1. Tap the Share button ⬆︎ at the bottom (or top) of Safari.
    2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
    3. Tap Add.

    On iPhone and iPad, the app keeps its own saved progress, separate from Safari.

    Android (Chrome)

    1. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
    2. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).
    3. Tap Install.

    Mac (Safari)

    1. Open the File menu.
    2. Click Add to Dock (needs macOS Sonoma or later).

    Computer (Chrome or Edge)

    1. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
    2. Click Install.
